The extension whitelist exists so a stray README, .txt or Thumbs.db in the folder doesn't get emitted as a broken . Upper-case variants are listed explicitly because cameras commonly write .JPG and Liquid's `contains` is case-sensitive. Deliberately NOT listed: .heic / .heif. iPhones shoot HEIC by default and only Safari renders it, so including it would show broken images in Chrome and Firefox. Convert those to JPEG or WebP first. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign allowed = ".jpg .jpeg .png .gif .webp .avif .svg .JPG .JPEG .PNG .GIF .WEBP .AVIF .SVG" | split: " " -%} {%- assign shots = site.static_files | where_exp: "f", "f.path contains prefix" | where_exp: "f", "allowed contains f.extname" | sort: "name" -%}
